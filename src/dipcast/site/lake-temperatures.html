Measured lake temperatures

Water temperatures the Environment Agency measured in English lakes, on the days it visited them between May and September. A record of past measurements by month, not today's temperature.

What these readings do not tell you

  • the temperature now
  • the temperature at the swim spot
  • a forecast
  • that water is safe to swim

How they were measured

The Environment Agency measured the water's temperature at a lake's sampling points on the days it visited them. A lake can have several sampling points; each reading here keeps its point and its date, and a point has at most one reading a day. Only readings from May to September were collected for this record, so it has no figures for October to April.

Source: the Environment Agency Water Quality Archive, water temperature (determinand 0076). Sampling points are matched to a lake when they lie inside its outline or on its water.
