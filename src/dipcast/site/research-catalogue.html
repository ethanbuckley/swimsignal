Research catalogue

Every research record SwimSignal keeps. Each says what its test found when it was run, kept as it was, and, apart from that, what is happening to the work now.

A failed test is kept with its scope. It says that one version did not help on those data, not that the idea cannot work. An archived version says what new data, mechanism or need would reopen it. Work that is in use or waiting for a set date is not abandoned.
