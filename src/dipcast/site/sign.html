Print this page on A4, portrait, and put it up where swimmers will see it, with the landowner's or the venue's agreement.

  • It gives no level: printed, a level would be out of date within hours. The QR code and the address open the spot's forecast, which is updated several times a day.
  • It needs no colour printer. Print at actual size; a printer's "fit to page" makes it slightly smaller, which still scans.
  • For a screen at the venue, the live sign shows the forecast and updates itself. The organisers' page lists the overflows upstream.
