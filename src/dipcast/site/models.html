Model scorecard

How a model reaches the forecast

  1. It is tested on past data, under rules written down before the scores are seen. A test that fails stays on record.
  2. It runs beside the live forecast for a fixed window, saving its own answers before anyone knows what the overflows will do. Nobody looks at its score until the agreed read date.
  3. On that date it is scored against the live model and a simple reference. A model that cannot beat them stays in the lab.
  4. Only then can a change to the forecast be proposed, in its own change, with a way back.
