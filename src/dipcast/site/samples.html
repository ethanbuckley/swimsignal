E. coli samples at bathing waters

Official lab samples of E. coli at bathing waters, each with its date: England's inland waters that this site covers, the Republic of Ireland's since 2014, and two Welsh rivers, at Hay-on-Wye and Llandeilo. A record of the water at those times, not today. For a water's rating and today's advice, its official page comes first; each water here links to it.

What these samples do not tell you

  • One sample is the water at one place and time. It says nothing about the water on another day, or later the same day.
  • One sample sets no rating. A rating uses four seasons of scheduled samples, by the rules of each country.
  • The hindsight estimate is not the forecast the site issued that day. The site's issued forecasts are scored on the Accuracy page.
  • A sample the model missed, or a warning with no high sample, does not point to any overflow, water company or other source.
  • In Wales and the Republic of Ireland SwimSignal makes no forecast and puts no estimate beside the samples. A high sample does not say where the bacteria came from.

How the hindsight estimate was made

After the samples were taken, the site's model was refitted once for each year, each time leaving out that year's samples, and run for each sample's day on the rain forecast archived for that day and the exposure to spills upstream, replayed for it. Its inputs are the ones the forecast uses: rain, the spills upstream, the season and, for rivers, how wet the soil was. The result is the chance the model gives of the count being over 900 per 100 ml. It was never shown on the site.

Wales and the Republic of Ireland

These samples were copied from each official body's published data on the date given at the top of the list, and do not update between copies. Each water's page says how its samples were measured and what "below" and "above" a limit mean. Where a sample is in both of Natural Resources Wales's sources, its Water Quality Archive and its bathing-water service, the two agree; the archive adds survey samples from 2023, and the service the last samples of 2026.
