Each spot's pollution risk forecast for the next five days, on one page to print before a session. The spots and the name are in the address, after the #, so they never reach a server.

SwimSignal is a free, non-commercial pollution risk forecast for river and lake swim spots in England, run by Ethan Buckley. A forecast, not a water test: check the signs at the water before you swim.
