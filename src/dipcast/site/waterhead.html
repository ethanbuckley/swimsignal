Waterhead bacteria samples

What these samples do not tell you

  • what the water is like now
  • what the water is like elsewhere in Windermere
  • a forecast
  • a bathing water rating, which is worked out from many samples over four years, never from one
  • that the water is safe to swim
  • how well SwimSignal's forecast did: these samples have not been compared with it

How they were taken

Volunteers from Ambleside Action for a Future sampled the water at Waterhead, at the head of Windermere, about once a week from spring to autumn. Waterhead is not a designated bathing water, so the Environment Agency does not sample it. Each count is what the sheet records, per 100 ml.

Sources

The counts are public domain and are shown here with the project's permission. The project's own write-up is Tim Boden's Waterhead E.coli Testing (WET) Project Technical Report (17 November 2025).
