SwimSignal forecasts listed rivers and lakes in England. For the coast, Wales and Scotland, use the official services below. Their observations and warnings answer different questions from our inland forecast.

Algae reports across the UK

The UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology (UKCEH) Bloomin' Algae map shows submitted reports. Read each report's date and verification category: a submitted or unverifiable record is not a confirmed harmful bloom. An absence of reports does not establish an absence of algae.

The algae observation shown on a SwimSignal spot page is the EA sampler's most recent visual check, where available. It does not identify toxic species. Broader Bloomin' Algae reports are available on the external map; they are not included in our risk level.

UKCEH's guidance and reporting app explain how to recognise and report suspected blooms. Follow local warnings and keep people and pets away from suspected blooms.

How coverage will grow

Regional observations will come first, labelled with their source, age and coverage. Forecasts require data we can reuse, a regional test on observations the model has never seen, and published evidence of useful performance. Coastal forecasts also require a model of tides and coastal transport.

The method is published, with live scores. An independent scientific review is still pending. See the development and validation plan.
