# Lake imagery: the derived-data contract, version 1

Written 31 October 2026 for #550, milestone 2: the research side of the visual satellite explorer. The design and
integration owner ("SwimSignal recurring decision experience") builds the viewer against this file. The pictures are
real Sentinel-2 data for the five pilot sites (`docs/SATELLITE-PILOT-2026-11.md`). They are made by
`scripts/satellite_imagery.py` in the weekly pilot workflow.

## What it may and may not say

The pictures show the satellite scenes the readings came from, and the index on the pixels each reading used. They
are pictures, not measurements of algae or toxins, and not swimming advice. A scene with no picture was too cloudy to
read: unknown, not clean. The index is drawn only on the reading's own open-water pixels within 300 m, never across
the whole lake. Out-of-season readings stay experimental (contract 1.1 of the lake observations). No picture is made,
filled or generated by anything but the satellite data.

## Files

- `data/research/lake_imagery.v1.json`, the manifest. The build publishes it as `data/research/lake_imagery.json`,
  with `available`.
- `data/research/lake-img/<lake id>/`:
  - `<item id>.png`: the true-colour picture;
  - `<item id>-mask.png`: the not-clear mask;
  - `<item id>-index.png`: the index, only for a shown reading;
  - `core.png`: the open water the reading uses.

  The build copies these as they are. Paths in the manifest are relative to `data/research/`.

## The manifest

- **Top level:** `schema` `swimsignal.lake-imagery`, `version`, `generated_at`, `attribution`, `method`, `claim`,
  `code_sha256`, `sites`, `runs`.
- **`method`:**
  - `bands`: image TCI (ESA's true-colour image of B4, B3, B2, one fixed scaling, 10 m); mask SCL (20 m); index NDCI
    at 20 m on clear core pixels;
  - `grid`: EPSG:4326, a square of radius 400 m around the site point, pixels of about 10 m (80 × 80), nearest-pixel
    resampling;
  - `index_legend`: min −0.2, max 0.4, five colours, values outside clipped;
  - `not_clear_colour`;
  - `scenes`: only the scenes the test's 80%-clear rule calls usable.
- **A site:** `lake_id`, `name`, `water_body`, `lat`, `lon`, `radius_m` (300, the reading's circle), `bbox` (west,
  south, east, north), `size_px`, `core_url`, `scenes`. **The bbox is the same for every scene of a site**, so any two
  dates line up exactly, whichever tile or UTM zone a scene came from. Barton Broad spans tiles 30UYD and 31UCU.
- **A scene,** in date order:
  - identity: `item_id`, `datetime` (capture, UTC), `tile`;
  - `checked_at` and `run_id`: the pilot run that read it (none for an earlier reading);
  - `source`: `current` (a new pilot scene) or `backfill` (an earlier shown reading);
  - `imaged_at` and `imaged_by`;
  - `image_url`, `mask_url`, `index_url` (null unless shown), each with its `_sha256`;
  - `quality`: `shown`, `shown_secondary` (a second image the same day), `excluded:<reason>` or `not_shown`;
  - `ndci`: the published reading, null unless shown;
  - `experimental`;
  - pixel counts: `index_pixels`, `index_median`, `core_pixels_in_window`, `clear_share_in_core`.
- **A run:** `run_id`, `started`, `finished`, `seconds`, `caps`, `requests`, `megabytes`, `made` (current, backfill),
  `current_due`, `backfill_remaining`, `errors`, `stop_reason`, `status`:
  - `ok`: every new scene got a picture;
  - `partial`: a cap or error stopped it before that;
  - `failed`: no new scene got one.

  A partial or failed run fails the workflow job after it is committed. Pictures already made are never deleted.

## Checks

`satellite_imagery.py check`, tested in `tests/test_satellite_imagery.py`, fails when:
- the schema or major version is wrong;
- a site's bbox does not hold its point;
- scenes are out of date order;
- a quality is not one of the listed values;
- an index value or index layer appears on a scene that is not shown;
- a drawn index median differs from the published reading by more than 0.01;
- an image is missing or its SHA-256 has changed;
- a run status is not one of the three.

## Cost (measured 31 October 2026, scratch run, no committed file touched)

| Read | Scenes | Requests | MiB | Seconds | Per scene |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B4, B3, B2 separately | 7 | 91 | 40.0 | 28 | 13 requests, 5.7 MiB |
| TCI + B4 (this route) | 8 | 85 | 40.0 | 30 | 10.6 requests, 5.0 MiB |
| TCI + B4, larger cap | 12 (10 new, 2 earlier) | 121 | 60.0 | 41 | 10.1 requests, 5.0 MiB |

- **Why so many bytes:** each band read fetches a whole 1024-pixel tile (about 1.5-1.7 MB) for an 80-pixel window.
  B4 at 10 m is still needed for the index.
- **Repository size:** about 24 KB of PNGs a scene (12 scenes: 292 KB).
- **Correctness:** every drawn index median matched the published reading within 0.001. Frensham was the furthest,
  0.0124 against 0.0132, because a few core pixels fall outside the window. The excluded Rutland scene of 27 Oct shows
  the cloud bank over the shore its exclusion describes.
- **Allocation:** the pilot's existing one, shared with the readings. Imagery takes at most 150 requests and 60 MiB,
  and never more than 300 requests and 100 MiB with that run's readings. The step has 8 minutes. A weekly run reads
  about 5-10 new usable scenes (25-50 MiB). The rest of the budget goes to earlier readings, newest first. At about 5
  of those a week, the 298 earlier shown readings at the five sites would take most of a year. The backfill can stop
  at any time with nothing lost.
- **Before more sites:** extend beyond the five only with this cost per scene in hand.
