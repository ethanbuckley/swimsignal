## 3. Next tasks, in order

Each is one PR against main unless it says otherwise.

**A. Make "right now" wait for travel time (model; changes headlines).**
`live_now_risk` in `src/dipcast/model/transport.py` counts a running spill at full weight from
the moment it starts, and decays a finished one from its end, whatever its `travel_h`. A spill
20 h upstream raises the spot at once and is gone too early. #97's `clear_time` already holds
each spill until its water has passed; the headline does not. Proposed rule: a spill counts
from start + travel time, holds until end + travel time, then decays with T90; the 48 h window
runs from when its water passed. A spill whose water has not arrived yet could say "arriving
in about N hours". Show, in the PR, how many of the 105 spots change level on the live data,
and get Ethan's yes there: on 4 Oct he approved a list that included this question, but not
this rule.

**B. Accuracy page wording.** The tile "93% Spill days ranked above dry days" is an AUC and sits
under "warned of 21% of spills"; a reader takes it for accuracy (and 93% is also NSW
Beachwatch's headline). Relabel it. Split #96's opening sentence into short ones.

phone map key ("offline or no feed") still use the old words. Use #98's `statusText` and

## 5. Rules that have bitten before

- Open every PR against main; never stack. Check `gh pr view N --json state` before pushing to
  a PR's branch: Ethan merges while work continues.
- Never run the pipeline, and never run several local builds at once: one IP got Open-Meteo 429s
  and EA 403s. Preview with the live data instead: copy `https://swimsignal.co.uk/data/` files
  into `site/data/` and call `build_site.write_pages`.
- Never dispatch `site.yml` or push to main to test runner behaviour; use a throwaway workflow
  on a throwaway branch.
- Other sessions work in sibling worktrees. Fetch main and read their uncommitted diffs before
  editing `site.yml` or `README.md`.
- Follow `docs/DESIGN.md`. Ethan judges by screenshots: before and after at 320, 375 and 1440 px.
  A level word always carries its noun ("High risk").
- Merging several PRs in quick succession gives two builds: the `site` concurrency group keeps one
  running and one waiting, and each new push replaces the waiting one.
