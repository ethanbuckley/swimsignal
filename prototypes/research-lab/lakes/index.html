A lake the model has never seen

At a lake it knew nothing about, could SwimSignal's lake model tell which days would read higher, and how high?

1

How to read it

read this first

Two questions, kept apart. The order: on the days a lake was sampled, did the model's higher figures fall on the days with higher counts? The level: did the counts land where the model said they would?

The order is a correlation inside each lake, averaged over lakes: 1 is perfect order, 0 is no better than chance. It does not depend on the lake's level, because a level moves every day at a lake by the same amount.

The level is read through the model's 8-in-10 range: the counts 8 in 10 samples should fall between. If it is right about level and spread, about 80 in 100 results fall inside. The bar, set before the tests, was 70 to 90 in 100 overall and at least 60 at every lake.

A lake it knows nothing about starts from a typical lake's level. A lake with its own past samples gets its own level from them. The model is otherwise the same.

What it changed on the site

At five lake bathing waters with no monitored overflow upstream (the Serpentine, Hampstead Heath's three ponds and Colwick), each lake's spot page gives, from May to September: the reading a sample would most likely show with its 8-in-10 range, on the lake's own level; and whether today's estimate is higher, about or lower than usual for that lake.

At 15 lake spots with no samples of their own, the range is no longer shown: a typical lake's range failed the bar. Seven English lakes among them get the higher-or-lower word only, because the order does not need the level. Two spots on lakes with sampled sites elsewhere take that lake's level.

None of this sets a level or a colour. The range on own levels, the typical-lake check, the word at unsampled lakes.

6

What it cannot tell you

  • Nothing about a lake nobody samples. The order was tested at sampled lakes. At an unsampled lake it is carried over, not checked.
  • Nothing about days ahead. Every test used rain as it fell, on the day of the sample.
  • Not "safe". A day low in a lake's order, or a reading near the bottom of its range, can still be high.
  • Few samples over 900. Too few to judge any chance of a result over 900 at these lakes.
  • Official advice comes first. Environment Agency notices and signs at the water are on each spot page of the live site.

Questions worth asking next

set, not yet read

  1. Does the range on own levels hold in 2027?

    Read after the season, on or after 15 October 2027, at the five lakes: 70 to 90 in 100 inside, as here.

  2. Does the higher-or-lower word keep its order in 2027?

    Samples on "higher" days should read above those on "lower" days, with an interval above 1.

  3. Can an unsampled lake be given a level at all?

    Useful if: a few samples at such a lake, taken on fixed days with a local group. Unconvincing if: another typical lake built from the same sampled ones.
