How to read it

read this first

A weight is the model's factor, from 0 to 1. For one overflow at one spot the forecast multiplies a survival factor (how long the model thinks the water takes to arrive) by a dilution factor. That product is the weight. It is not the share of a spill that arrives, and nobody measured it.

The ledger adds the weights up. For each overflow: how many listed spots the model traces it to, its weights added over those spots, and its largest weight at one spot. "Weighted spills a year" multiplies the summed weight by the Environment Agency's long-term average spill count for the overflow.

How far to trust the chain.

What no row says. No row says an overflow caused a sample result, put any share of the bacteria at a spot, or broke a permit. The model holds no spill volume, and no treated sewage, farm or road runoff.
