What it cannot tell you

  • It is the model's answer, not the river's. Every figure here is what the production spill model gives when its inputs change. Nobody measured it.
  • Taking an overflow away is not an upgrade. It means "had it never spilled". An upgraded overflow can still spill, and storage at one overflow can push water to another. The model has no link between overflows, so it cannot show that.
  • Scaled rain is not a climate. Every hour is multiplied by the same number. A wetter year would bring longer, more intense and more frequent storms, not the same storms scaled.
  • Past the wettest days it has seen, the model gives a flat answer. Section 5 says how often the scaled rain went past the wettest day in the cache.
  • Nothing here changes a forecast. No issued forecast, model setting or evaluation was touched. Official advice at the water comes first.
